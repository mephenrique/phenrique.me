## The reaction to the fall of someone who hurt us

Start from [Raphael Abdalla's reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgGbxG8R0La/) from November 23, 2026. In the caption, he relates being born again to the way we react when someone who wronged us falls. The desire for vengeance gives way to compassion. The question I want to explore is what is lost when that person falls.

## What we lose together

- **The image of God.** Develop how the dignity of the other remains even when they hurt us. What unique contributions of their life might be lost when they cease to flourish?
- **Shared suffering.** In 1 Corinthians 12:26, Paul speaks of the suffering of one member of the body of Christ as the suffering of all. Explore how this image can illuminate, by analogy, our interdependence even outside the church.

## Desiring restoration

Explore how desiring the restoration of someone who hurt us can go hand in hand with acknowledging the harm and seeking justice. Close with a question: what good could reach others if the one whose fall we are tempted to celebrate were to rise again?
